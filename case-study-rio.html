Case study

RIO Furnitures

Seating and tables made to order in a Nairobi workshop — online, and findable.

  • Web development
  • E-commerce
  • JavaScript
  • Supabase
  • PostgreSQL

The problem

RIO makes seating and tables to order for restaurants, bars, offices and outdoor spaces. The work is good and the workshop is busy, but online the business was close to invisible: no site of its own, and an out-of-date Google listing doing the talking. Enquiries arrived one WhatsApp message at a time, each starting from scratch.

Goals

  • Show the range properly, with real photographs of real pieces
  • Turn scattered WhatsApp questions into structured quote requests
  • Let the owner update prices and content without calling me
  • Make the business findable in search

My role

Sole developer and designer: visual direction, build, database, deployment and domain, plus the product photography and the search and business-listing setup.

Architecture

A multi-page JavaScript front end deployed on GitHub Pages behind a custom domain, with Supabase (PostgreSQL) holding the data: product and price list, quote requests, site-visit bookings and customer reviews, with auth separating the public site from the admin screens.

Implementation

  • Catalogue by category, with piece counts so the range reads at a glance
  • Quote flow capturing contact details and project context up front
  • A site-visit booking page for customers who want to see the workshop
  • Admin dashboard: editable price list, reviews the owner publishes herself
  • Photography shot and prepared in-house, with a made-to-order note on varying pieces
  • Titles, business schema, sitemap and Search Console verification

Challenges

Two worth recording. The quote form originally let people submit without usable contact details, which made the lead worthless — fixed by requiring and validating contact information before submit. And the domain went down mid-project after a registrar verification deadline passed; diagnosing that taught me more about DNS than the build did.
